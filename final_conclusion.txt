QUESTION (c) - FINAL CONCLUSION

Both Max Heap and Linear Search correctly found the highest score, 95.

For one maximum search, Linear Search is simple and requires 7 comparisons for
the given eight scores. It does not need a separate heap construction.

A Max Heap requires construction, which used 12 parent-child comparisons in
this implementation. However, after construction, the maximum is always at
the root and can be obtained in O(1) time. A new score can be inserted in
O(log n) time in the worst case.

Therefore, for a one-time maximum search, Linear Search is a simple choice.
When scores are continuously inserted and the highest score must be maintained
and accessed repeatedly, a Max Heap is more suitable.
